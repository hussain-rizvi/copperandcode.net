06 / HOW WE WORK

From idea to manufacturable product

A sequence, not a scramble. Every stage produces something you can review before we move to the next.

01

Scope

We map the product, its constraints, and the real risks before writing a line of code or drawing a single trace — so nothing expensive surprises us in month three.

02

Architect

System architecture across hardware, firmware, cloud, and apps — decided together, because every layer constrains the others.

03

Build

Boards, firmware, backend, and apps developed in parallel against shared interfaces, so integration is a formality, not a fire drill.

04

Integrate & test

Boards brought up, then the whole system tested end to end — device, cloud, and app — against real conditions, with the data to prove it.

05

Handoff

Manufacturable design files, source code, documentation, and deployed software — packaged so your team, or your manufacturer, can take it and scale.
